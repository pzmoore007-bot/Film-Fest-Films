THE PROBLEM

Great films should not disappear when the festival ends.

Every year, exceptional independent films are selected by respected film festivals, screened before audiences, praised by critics—and then struggle to receive meaningful distribution.

Film Fest Films is designed to close that gap by turning festival recognition into paid distribution, sustained visibility, audience discovery, and commercial opportunity.

THE MODEL

Festival discovery becomes commercial momentum.

01

Festival Selection

A participating festival becomes the first quality filter.

02

Film Fest Films Acquisition

Selected titles may receive a proposed $10,000 Minimum Guarantee in exchange for defined limited rights.

03

90-Day First Look

The film enters a focused post-festival window for audiences, institutions, and approved industry professionals.

04

Distribution or Extended Run

A larger buyer can acquire the film—or Film Fest Films can continue distribution under a revenue-sharing model.

FOR FILMMAKERS

Selected. Paid. Distributed. Discovered.

We do not believe filmmakers should have to pay yet another fee simply to be seen. The Film Fest Films model puts capital behind the movies we select.

$10,000

Proposed Minimum Guarantee

Selected filmmakers receive guaranteed upfront compensation. Film Fest Films assumes the commercial risk and works to recoup through the film's defined revenue streams.

  • No listing fee for acquired titles
  • Defined rights and limited term
  • Marketing and filmmaker interviews
  • Industry discovery opportunities
  • Potential revenue participation after recoupment
